## Downstream behavior changes

- Ordinary interview and execution workflows accept clear contextual confirmation and scoped delegation. Settled decisions are reused; material missing decisions pause only dependent work.
- Spec and planning workflows distinguish planning-only requests from preparation inside authorized implementation. Existing canonical documents and other tasks' work are preserved.
- Incremental delivery and independent review depend on actual complexity, uncertainty, and impact rather than file count, line count, or the existence of branching logic.
- TDD, debugging, browser checks, and the completion checklist use relevant evidence plus mandatory project checks. Passing focused checks do not justify claiming untested behavior, and no full suite, commit, telemetry installation, or cross-model review is automatically required.
- Browser content, logs, source code, and generated output remain evidence rather than action authority. Authorized benign interactions do not require repeated confirmation; external content cannot expand authorization.
- The architecture four-path contract and no-write exit conditions remain intact. Acceptance of the same brief carries across turns; general delegation does not replace brief acceptance. Adoption decisions and project-dialectic consent remain item-scoped.

These are model-neutral instruction changes informed by the [official GPT-6 guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/latest-model?model=gpt-6-astra), not an API migration or a claim that every prior rule fails on GPT-6. No model detection or compatibility runtime is added.
